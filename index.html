Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting the best movie recommendations

What is Moview? +

Moview (e-moview) is a free community-driven movie recommendation website where real people give genuine film suggestions based on your mood, preferred genre, and language. Explore curated lists or ask for personalized recommendations.

How do I find what movie to watch tonight on Moview? +

You can post a recommendation request stating your mood, preferred genre, or language, browse our curated catalog of 200+ Top Movies, or explore active community requests to see what film lovers recommend.

Does Moview offer recommendations across different languages? +

Yes! Moview features recommendations across English (Hollywood), Hindi (Bollywood), Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Marathi, Korean cinema, Japanese anime, French, Spanish, and more.

What are the best movies to watch right now? +

Top trending movies include anticipated titles like The Odyssey, Project Hail Mary, Drishyam 3, Demon Slayer Infinity Castle, and popular classics like Interstellar, Parasite, and The Dark Knight.

How do I get streaming recommendations for Netflix and Prime Video? +

When asking for a recommendation on Moview, you can mention your streaming service such as Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar, or Hulu in your request description to get suggestions available on your specific platform.
